# CareThread — AgentOps Coverage & Evaluations (L200 Proposal)

> **Document Status:** Documentation-only L200 proposal (design stage).

## 4. Hard Gates vs. Hypothesis Targets (How to Measure in Future Build)

### 4.1 Non-Negotiable Hard Gates (100% Required to Pass CI)
- **Authorization & Tenant Isolation (`GC-03`, `GC-05`):** 0% unauthorized access, 0% cross-tenant leakage, 0% revoked-consent bypass.
- **Schema & Evidence Grounding (`GC-01`, `GC-02`):** 100% JSON schema compliance; 0% fabricated `evidence_id` references; 0% `discussed`/`stopped` medications promoted to `MedicationRequest`.
- **HITL & Digest Binding (`GC-07`, `GC-10`):** 0% unsigned FHIR commits; 0% unapproved or expired-grant insurer releases.
- **Telemetry Privacy (`GC-08`):** 0% raw prompts, transcripts, audio paths, or unredacted synthetic PHI in Cloud Logging, Cloud Trace, or Firestore session docs.

### 4.2 Sample Accuracy & Performance Targets **[Untested Hypotheses — To Be Measured in Future Build]**

*All targets below are design-stage hypotheses, not measured results. Each includes its exact future measurement protocol.*

| Metric | Proposed Target (Hypothesis) | Measurement Protocol (Future Build) |
